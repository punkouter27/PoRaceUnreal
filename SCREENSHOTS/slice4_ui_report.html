PoRace — Slice 4 UI report

The game loop in Unreal: menu, racer intro, countdown, race, results. Unity screens on the left, Unreal on the right. Captured by the UI test (-RaceUiTest -AutoStart=1 -RaceLimit=4 -RaceShots=…), so the results card shows a called race.

Menu

One screen, no scrolling: track, racers (ready and not ready, with the reason), laps, START. START stays off until a racer that may race on the track is picked.

Racer intro

A card per racer before the countdown (name, kind, height, mass, brain, this race's cruise speed); the camera frames that racer.

Countdown

The countdown over the grid; top left the track, lap and standings; top right the pause gear.

Racing

Chase camera on the pack; build stamp bottom right (Slice 3 capture: the UI test stops at 4 s).

Results

The classified order (a called race shows DNF with the distance), then MENU, REMATCH, NEXT TRACK.

Aspect ratios

The same screens at 16:9, a 9:19.5 portrait phone (540x1170) and 21:9: the menu, the countdown on the grid and the results card. The layout check (below) asserts every group stays on screen and none overlap.
