What happened, in plain words

  1. m25 tested speed-scaled stride frequency on Matt (202M steps). Raising stride frequency +0.2 Hz per m/s above 1.2 m/s held 95/100 race graduation and 10/10 ×3 Toddler, but 2.5 m/s jogging still lacked an aerial flight phase. Kept matt_m20 (98/100) as production.
  2. g1 learned to fall over at once (0–202M). For an untrained body every second alive cost −0.44 reward/step while falling cost −2 once, so ending the episode in 3.7 steps (0.07 s) was the mathematical optimum.
  3. g2 learned to sit on the floor (0–32M). Flooring step reward at zero stopped early suicide, but stock termination only checked for an upside-down torso, so sitting upright on the floor scored 978/1000 steps with 0 foot force.
  4. g3 stood and passed Toddler 10/10 ×3, but stomped at 20× body weight (0–64M). Adding a pelvis fall-height termination (0.54 m) forced her to stand, passing Toddler at 42.6M, but the reward floor hid the penalty of 20 BW foot impacts.
  5. g4 stomped harder (76 BW); g5 became smooth (0.73 BW) but kneeled. Restoring full contact-force penalty and adding action-rate smoothing (−0.1) in g5 eliminated stomping immediately (jerk 0.009), but she discovered kneeling on one knee/hand above 0.54 m.
  6. g6–g7 stood cleanly like Matt (77M–332M lineage). Adding low-body height termination (knees/elbows/wrists/torso > 0.15 m) closed the kneeling exploit. She stood 10/10 at 1.04 BW and survived walking shoves 10/10, while PPO policy/value/entropy loss logging was enabled.
  7. g8 collapsed on an abrupt alive-bonus cut; g9 stabilized at lr 1e-4. Cutting alive 15 → 3 in one jump shocked the value critic; stepping alive 15 → 8 at lr 1e-4 in g9 restored 9/10 standing and 10/10 walking-push recovery, but she stood still (0.02 m/s) when commanded to walk.
  8. g10 unlocked walking (0.02 → 0.50 m/s) and first graduation passes. Boosting wide linear-velocity tracking 1 → 4 and easing alive 8 → 5 broke the standing local optimum, cutting walking velocity error from 0.49 → 0.20 m/s.
  9. g12–g13 closed in on graduation. She passes the toddler tier every time now, and graduation climbed 27 → 52 → 71 → 84 of 100 episodes. The child tier is held back by a limp that comes from her skeleton: the scan rig's left and right legs differ (thigh 29.2 vs 27.6 cm).
  10. g14 graduated. A gentle fine-tune (half the learning rate) of g13's best checkpoint passed the race graduation test: 94/100 episodes, and 91/100 on fresh seeds. Her policy was exported to ONNX.

Glossary

  • Episode: One training attempt, up to 1000 control steps (20 s at 50 Hz). It ends early when the agent falls or touches a knee/elbow/hand to the floor.
  • Eval reward: Total score of an evaluation episode across 128 parallel test worlds. Higher within a fixed reward recipe indicates stronger convergence.
  • Checkpoint: Saved policy + value network weights at a specific step count; saved to training/final/ when a milestone gate is set.
  • Toddler tier: Skill ladder Level 1: Stand still 10 s (10/10), survive a 30 N·s lateral shove standing (≥9/10) and walking (≥9/10) with zero non-foot floor contacts.
  • Child tier: Skill ladder Level 2: Walk smoothly from standstill at 0.4, 0.8, and 1.2 m/s with velocity error < 0.20 m/s, stance asymmetry < 0.05, and foot force < 3.0 BW.
  • Graduation: 100 episodes of randomized race speed and turn commands; an agent must pass ≥90/100 episodes with 0 NaN gradients before ONNX export.
  • Body weight (BW): 99th-percentile foot impact force divided by body weight (722 N for Grandma, 879 N for Matt). ~1.0–1.2 BW standing and ~2.0 BW walking is human-like.
  • Jerk: Mean squared change in joint actions between 50 Hz steps; lower values (~0.007–0.011) produce smooth, natural motion without high-frequency jitter.
  • PPO losses: Learner health signals: policy loss (surrogate update magnitude), value loss (critic prediction error), and entropy loss (negative exploration entropy).
