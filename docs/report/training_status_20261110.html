The three main training signals

These are the three numbers training logs most closely (in TensorBoard), shown for Blue's everyday racing brain, the one every race uses. Read them like a learner's report card. Score is the overall mark the learner is chasing. Seconds without falling shows whether it stays on its feet for the whole 20 s test. Speed-following points show whether it goes the speed it's told. Hover over a dot to see its value.

All three curves rise, then go flat. A flat line is the signal to stop: more practice no longer helps, so the run was ended and the brain was put through its skill tests.
